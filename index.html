Two Services

Custom build or a fresh face for your existing kitchen.

Most cabinet shops in Green Bay run one lane. Showcase Kitchens runs two: a full custom build for new construction and gut remodels, and a dedicated Renew-A-Kitchen refacing division for homeowners who want a brand-new kitchen look without replacing the boxes.

Division One

Custom Cabinetry

Every cabinet designed and built in the Yeager Drive shop, from your first concept sketch through final install. Baked-on finishes that hold up to real Wisconsin kitchen use, with countertop work included.

  • Full custom design and build, start to finish
  • Baked-on finishes for kitchen-grade durability
  • Countertops and integrated millwork
  • Residential and commercial projects
  • New construction and full gut remodels
Best for: new builds and full remodels
Division Two

Renew-A-Kitchen Refacing

The in-house refacing brand inside Showcase Kitchens. New doors, new drawer fronts, and a full refinish of the existing boxes, so your kitchen looks completely new without the cost or timeline of a rebuild.

  • New cabinet doors and drawer fronts
  • Refinish and refresh of existing boxes
  • Faster turnaround than a full custom build
  • Distinct pricing tier, same quality craftsmanship
  • Great for kitchens where the layout already works
Best for: layout stays, look needs updating

Our Story

Scott Karbon and 50 years of built-in Green Bay.

Scott Karbon Owner, Showcase Kitchens Inc

Showcase Kitchens Inc opened on Yeager Drive in 1976. Fifty years on, Scott Karbon runs the same shop the same way: every cabinet designed and built in Green Bay, with baked-on finishes made to hold up to real Wisconsin kitchens.

"Half a century of custom cabinetry built right here in Green Bay. That's the standard we hold every project to."

The work divides cleanly into two lanes. The first is full custom cabinetry for new construction and gut remodels, taking a job from the first rendering all the way through install. The second is Renew-A-Kitchen, the in-house refacing brand for homeowners who want a fully refreshed kitchen without tearing the boxes out.

Showcase Kitchens covers countertops, residential and commercial work, kitchens and baths, and the full design-to-install process. Scott is active on LinkedIn and the team shares project work regularly on Instagram, where the shop has built a community following since 1976.

How It Works

From first conversation to finished kitchen.

A clear four-step process so you always know where the project stands.

01

Design Consultation

Come into the showroom or schedule a site visit. Scott walks through your space, your goals, and your budget, then builds a 3D rendering so you can see it before anything is cut.

02

Material Selection

Choose door styles, wood species, finish colors, and countertop options from the showroom samples. For Renew-A-Kitchen jobs, new door profiles and veneers are selected the same way.

03

Built in Green Bay

Every cabinet is fabricated in the Yeager Drive shop. Baked-on finishes applied in-house. No outsourcing, no drop-shipping, no surprises with quality.

04

Professional Install

The Showcase Kitchens team handles delivery and install. Countertops coordinated and installed in the same project so the kitchen comes together as one finished piece.
